Everything your grounds need, under one contract

Weekly care, irrigation, trees, improvements and storm response, handled by one crew that knows your property and reports to you after every walkthrough.

Grounds maintenance

Scheduled visits that keep turf, shrubs and hardscape consistent from week to week. Your crew follows a written site plan, so the same standard is met whether it's January or July.

Crew edging turf along a community sidewalk at sunrise.
  • Turf careMowing, edging, string trimming and blowing on a set schedule, with seasonal mowing heights.
  • Shrub and groundcover careNatural-form pruning that keeps desert plants healthy and flowering instead of sheared into boxes.
  • Weed controlPre-emergent in fall and late winter, plus spot treatment in granite, beds and cracks.
  • Litter and debrisPickup across beds, islands and common areas on every visit.
  • Granite and hardscapeRaking, edges kept clean, and thin areas flagged for top-dressing.
  • Visit scheduleWeekly or biweekly, set in your contract.

Irrigation and water management

Water is the largest landscape cost on most Phoenix properties, and the most common source of plant loss. We manage your system as a whole, adjusting it through the year and catching leaks before they show up on the water bill.

Technician adjusting a smart controller in an irrigation cabinet.
  • Controller programmingRun times set by zone and season, adjusted as temperatures change.
  • System checksZone-by-zone inspection for broken heads, clogged emitters, leaks and overspray.
  • RepairsValves, heads, drip lines and wiring, with routine fixes covered under your contract terms.
  • Water reviewUsage compared month to month, with recommendations when a zone is using more than it should.
  • UpgradesSmart controllers, drip conversions and rain sensors, quoted before any work begins.

Tree and palm care

Healthy trees shade parking lots and walkways, and badly pruned ones are the first to fall in a monsoon. We prune for structure and long-term health, and we schedule the work around Phoenix's storm season.

Structural pruning on a mesquite near a pool deck.
  • Structural pruningThinning and raising canopies to reduce wind load and keep clearance over walks and drives.
  • Pre-monsoon thinningScheduled in late spring so trees go into storm season lighter and better balanced.
  • Palm trimmingFronds, seed pods and skinning in summer, after flowering.
  • Tree healthWatching for stress, pests and lean, with written recommendations when something needs attention.

Enhancements and installs

Improvements that raise the look and value of your property, from replacing a few plants to converting turf to desert landscape. As a licensed Arizona contractor (ROC 355147), we can take on installation work beyond routine maintenance.

Before and after of a refreshed community entry bed.
  • Plant replacementReplacing dead or declining plants with species suited to the site's sun and water.
  • Granite refreshTop-dressing thin areas and matching existing color and size.
  • Turf conversionReplacing high-water turf with low-water desert plantings and drip irrigation.
  • Entry and amenity upgradesNew plantings for entries, monuments, pool areas and courtyards.
  • How it's pricedA written proposal with scope, plant list and price, approved by you before we start.

Monsoon and storm response

Phoenix storms arrive fast and leave a mess. Preparation happens months ahead, and when a storm does hit, your property is on our list for the next morning.

Crew clearing a fallen limb from a parking lot after a storm.
  • Before the seasonTree thinning, clearing drainage and basin inlets, and securing young trees.
  • After a stormDebris cleared from drives, walks and parking first, then beds and turf.
  • Damage reportPhotos of anything broken or uprooted, with a proposal for repairs or replacement.
  • Response timeOn site the morning after a major storm.
