Landscape services for HOAs and commercial properties

Each service is available on its own or as part of a maintenance contract, and the crew assigned to your property handles the work.

Grounds maintenance

Scheduled visits for turf, shrubs and hardscape. Your crew follows a written plan for the property, with mowing heights, pruning and weed control adjusted by season.

  • Turf careMowing, edging, string trimming and blowing on a set schedule, with seasonal mowing heights for and winter rye.
  • Shrub and groundcover care that keeps desert plants healthy and flowering.
  • Weed control in fall and late winter, plus spot treatment in granite, beds and cracks.
  • Litter and debrisPickup across beds, islands and common areas on every visit.
  • Granite and hardscapeRaking, edges kept clean, and thin areas flagged for top-dressing.
  • Visit scheduleWeekly or biweekly, set in your contract.

Irrigation and water management

Water is usually the largest landscape expense on a Phoenix property and the most common cause of plant loss. We program, inspect and repair your system through the year and compare water use from month to month.

  • Controller programmingRun times set by zone and season, adjusted as temperatures change.
  • System checksZone-by-zone inspection for broken heads, clogged emitters, leaks and overspray.
  • RepairsValves, heads, drip lines and wiring, with routine fixes covered under your contract terms.
  • Water reviewUsage compared month to month, with recommendations when a zone is using more than it should.
  • UpgradesSmart controllers, drip conversions and rain sensors, quoted before any work begins.

Tree and palm care

Pruning for structure and long-term health keeps shade over parking and walkways and reduces storm damage. We schedule thinning ahead of the season.

  • Structural pruning to reduce wind load and keep clearance over walks and drives.
  • Pre-monsoon thinningScheduled in late spring so trees go into storm season lighter and better balanced.
  • Palm trimmingFronds, seed pods and skinning in summer, after flowering.
  • Tree healthWatching for stress, pests and lean, with written recommendations when something needs attention.

Enhancements and installs

Improvements to the look and value of your property, from replacing a few plants to converting turf to desert landscape. Premier is a licensed Arizona contractor (ROC 355147) and handles installation projects as well as maintenance.

  • Plant replacementReplacing dead or declining plants with species suited to the site's sun and water.
  • Granite refreshTop-dressing thin areas and matching existing color and size.
  • Turf conversionReplacing high-water turf with low-water desert plantings and irrigation.
  • Entry and amenity upgradesNew plantings for entries, monuments, pool areas and courtyards.
  • How it's pricedA written proposal with scope, plant list and price, approved by you before we start.

Monsoon and storm response

Storm preparation starts in late spring with tree thinning and drainage work. After a major storm, your property is on the next morning's schedule.

  • Before the seasonTree thinning, clearing drainage and basin inlets, and securing young trees.
  • After a stormDebris cleared from drives, walks and parking first, then beds and turf.
  • Damage reportPhotos of anything broken or uprooted, with a proposal for repairs or replacement.
  • Response timeOn site the morning after a major storm.

BermudaThe warm-season grass on most Phoenix turf. It grows through summer and turns brown and dormant in winter.
Drip irrigationSmall tubes and emitters that deliver water slowly to each plant's roots. Used for shrubs, trees and beds.
Natural-form pruningPruning that keeps a plant's own shape by removing selected branches at their base.
Structural pruningPruning that builds a strong trunk and well-spaced branches, so the tree holds up in wind.
MonsoonArizona's summer storm season, June 15 to September 30, with high winds, dust storms and heavy rain.
TransitionThe late-spring period when winter ryegrass dies back in the heat and bermuda grows in again.
