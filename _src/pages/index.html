Seasonal work in Phoenix

Desert landscapes follow a yearly schedule. We plan each property's seasonal work in advance and confirm it with you before it starts.

Fall

September to November

  • Scalp bermuda and overseed winter rye
  • Pre-emergent for winter weeds
  • Shorter irrigation run times as it cools
  • Planting while the soil is still warm

Winter

December to February

  • Frost protection for tender plants
  • Dormant pruning of deciduous trees
  • Controllers set to winter schedules
  • Spring pre-emergent in February

Spring

March to May

  • Transition from rye back to bermuda
  • Full irrigation check before the heat
  • Feed bermuda as it greens up
  • Pre-monsoon tree thinning begins

Summer

June to August

  • Early crew starts for heat safety
  • Palm trimming after flowering
  • Longer run times and frequent drip checks
  • Monsoon storm response and cleanup
