Properties we serve

HOA boards, commercial property owners and multifamily managers each report to different people. We set up each contract around the people you report to.

HOA communities

Board members change, community managers handle many communities at once, and residents report small problems quickly. We keep records the board can use and route requests through your manager.

Community entry monument with fresh plantings and clean granite.
Board meetings
Walkthrough reports written to be shared with the board as-is, with photos and plain recommendations.
Resident requests
Requests go through your community manager, and we confirm with your manager when each one is done.
Common areas
Entries, parks, paths, retention basins and pool areas on a consistent schedule.
Budgets
Enhancement proposals sized to your reserve and operating budgets, prioritized so the board can phase work.

Parking and shade

Water costs

One point of contact
