Properties we serve

HOA boards, commercial property owners and multifamily managers each report to different people. We set up each contract around the people you report to.

HOA communities

Board members change, community managers handle many communities at once, and residents report small problems quickly. We keep records the board can use and route requests through your manager.

Board meetings
Walkthrough reports written to be shared with the board as-is, with photos and plain recommendations.
Resident requests
Requests go through your community manager, and we confirm with your manager when each one is done.
Common areas
Entries, parks, paths, retention basins and pool areas on a consistent schedule.
Budgets
Enhancement proposals sized to your reserve and operating budgets, prioritized so the board can phase work.

Office and business parks

Grounds are part of what tenants pay for, and visitors see them before they reach the lobby. Consistent upkeep supports lease renewals.

Quiet service
Loud work scheduled around business hours where your site needs it.
Parking and shade
Parking lot islands kept tidy and trees pruned for shade and clearance over cars.
Water costs
Irrigation reviewed regularly so water use stays predictable across your portfolio.
One point of contact
A single account lead for every property you manage with us.

Retail and mixed-use

Customers form an impression of a center while they park. Clean lots, trimmed islands and visible signs help your tenants keep their customers.

Early starts
Crews on site before stores open, so the lot is clean when customers arrive.
Sightlines
Plantings kept low around signs, entries and drive aisles for visibility and safety.
Litter
Pickup across islands, perimeters and behind buildings on every visit.
Seasonal color
Entry color changes quoted ahead of the holiday and spring seasons.

Multifamily

Prospective residents tour the grounds before they see a unit, and current residents walk them every day when deciding whether to renew.

Tour-ready grounds
Leasing paths, pool decks and courtyards kept to the highest standard on the property.
Resident safety
Paths clear, trees pruned away from lighting, and chemicals applied with posted notice.
Make-ready support
Quick turnarounds on patio and unit-adjacent landscaping when units turn over.
Durable plantings
Plant choices that handle foot traffic, pets and Phoenix heat.

OverseedSpreading ryegrass seed over dormant bermuda in fall, so the turf stays green through winter.
ScalpMowing bermuda very short in September, so new ryegrass seed can reach the soil.
Pre-emergentA weed treatment applied before weed seeds sprout. It keeps them from starting to grow.
BermudaThe warm-season grass on most Phoenix turf. It grows through summer and turns brown and dormant in winter.
Drip irrigationSmall tubes and emitters that deliver water slowly to each plant's roots. Used for shrubs, trees and beds.
Natural-form pruningPruning that keeps a plant's own shape by removing selected branches at their base.
Structural pruningPruning that builds a strong trunk and well-spaced branches, so the tree holds up in wind.
MonsoonArizona's summer storm season, June 15 to September 30, with high winds, dust storms and heavy rain.
TransitionThe late-spring period when winter ryegrass dies back in the heat and bermuda grows in again.
